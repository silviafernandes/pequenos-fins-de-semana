Family outings and municipal collection
=======================================

Opening-hour visits live in `events.json.outings`, separately from dated activity sessions. They render under “Ao vosso ritmo”, without a guessed one-hour duration, nap label or calendar session export. An outing requires an exact venue within 15 km, official all-ages admission evidence, explicit weekend hours, a date range overlapping an upcoming weekend and a successful check within 48 hours. Prices stay unknown unless explicitly published. Only the general visit is recommended; rides, workshops and special activities need their own age checks.

Scheduled sources now cover SEA LIFE Porto, Maia's municipal Christmas market, ANDARTE's Matosinhos Christmas programme, Zoo da Maia, Matosinhos city hall and Porto/Ágora. The SEA LIFE and Maia adapters parse the official pages every run; changed or missing essential details remove an outing from publication. Annual market dates are not rolled into a new year. Holiday exceptions and the earlier end of Maia's craft market are disclosed.

Matosinhos market and Zoo da Maia remain under review because the current official information does not explicitly establish admission conditions for under-threes. The zoo's published child price starting at three is a price band, not evidence of either a three-year minimum or free toddler admission. Municipal programme announcements do not establish age eligibility for every attraction. Source status reports these gaps rather than fabricating events.

Regressions cover old years, changed hours, missing ages, stale and future evidence, exact venue/radius checks, weekday-only date ranges, failures and deduplication. Browser checks cover the distinct outing cards and absence of session calendar controls.
